About Healspring

What is Healspring Physio LLP?

Healspring Physio LLP is a Pan-India physiotherapy equipment distributor based in India, supplying premium medical rehabilitation devices to physiotherapists, orthopaedic surgeons, sports medicine professionals, and rehabilitation centres across North India and Pan-India. Founded by Mukesh Ahirwar and Pravesh Gautam, Healspring holds direct partnerships with 6 leading global manufacturers and brings world-class equipment to modern clinics across 140+ countries' worth of clinical-grade device networks.

Healspring refers to the combination of healing and clinical spring — representing the growth and recovery outcomes the company's equipment enables. With 8+ years of domain expertise, the company bridges international physiotherapy innovation and Indian clinical practice, offering end-to-end support from demo to installation to after-sales service.
