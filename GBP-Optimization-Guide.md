## 3. Photo Strategy

Google ranks profiles higher when photos are regularly added. Photos also directly drive more calls and direction requests.

### Priority Photos to Upload Now
1. **Logo** — High-res Healspring logo on white background
2. **Cover photo** — Best product image (the dual NeuroStim chair setup is visually striking)
3. **All 10 product images** from `ToUseImgs/` — label each before upload
4. **Founders photo** (`Founders image.png`) — builds trust
5. **Equipment in use** — if you have any clinical demo photos, add them

### Ongoing Photo Cadence
- Add at least **2 new photos per week**
- Label photos descriptively before uploading (e.g., `AI9-exoskeleton-robot-healspring.jpg`)
- Use real photos, not stock images — Google's algorithm detects this

### Photo Naming Convention (before upload)
```
healspring-[product-name]-[city]-physiotherapy.jpg
Example: healspring-neurostim-chair-delhi-physiotherapy.jpg
```

## 5. Review Strategy

Reviews are the #1 local ranking factor after relevance. Target: 20+ reviews in 90 days.

### Who to Ask
- Past clients / distributors who bought equipment
- Physiotherapists who attended a demo even if they didn't buy
- Colleagues and professional connections in the physio field

### How to Ask
1. **WhatsApp message** (highest response rate):
   > "Hi [Name], hope the [Equipment] is working well for your patients! Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It really helps us reach more clinics across India. Here's the direct link: [your GBP review link] — takes less than 2 minutes. Thank you!"

2. **Get your review link:** Go to your GBP → Get more reviews → Copy link
   - Your current profile: `https://share.google/B3f509khfomi6Q3Zl`
   - Look for the review shortcut link in your GBP dashboard

### Review Response Template

**For positive reviews:**
> Thank you so much, [Name]! We're thrilled that [clinic name] has been happy with the [product]. It's always wonderful to hear that our equipment is making a difference for your patients. We look forward to supporting you further. — Team Healspring

**For neutral/negative reviews:**
> Thank you for your feedback, [Name]. We take all feedback seriously and would love to understand your experience better. Please reach out to us directly at [phone/email] so we can resolve this promptly. — Team Healspring

**Never:** ignore a review, delete a negative one, or respond defensively.

## 8. Local SEO Signals Beyond GBP

GBP is the anchor, but these amplify your ranking.

### Citation Building (Free)
Submit your business to these directories with **identical** Name / Address / Phone (NAP):
- Justdial: justdial.com
- Sulekha: sulekha.com
- IndiaMart: indiamart.com (especially important for B2B equipment)
- TradeIndia: tradeindia.com
- Healthgrades / Practo (if listed in healthcare)
- Yellow Pages India

**NAP Consistency is critical:** Even minor variations (e.g., "LLP" vs "Pvt Ltd") hurt rankings.

### IndiaMART Profile (High Priority)
IndiaMART is where hospitals and procurement managers search for medical equipment suppliers. Create a free seller profile, list all products with photos and price ranges. This also feeds into Google's local signals.

### WhatsApp Business Profile
- Set up a WhatsApp Business account linked to the same phone number as GBP
- Add a product catalog with photos
- Set automated greeting and away messages
- Include your GBP link in the business description
